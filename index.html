WHY THIS GETS MEASURED

Measured, not eyeballed

This isn't about being careful for its own sake — a caption that drifts or audio that clips is a clip that comes back for a redo, and a redo is days you don't get back. So the same two checks run on every edit before it ships, not eyeballed, not a one-off for this page. Ask any editor for their own numbers on these two things — most won't have an answer, because most don't measure.

01

Caption position

Measured with
style_probe.py — samples the caption's bounding box across 40 frames of the rendered clip and logs its Y-position in pixels.
Measured on
Every clip we ship, and the same check run on a comparable clip from another editor's public portfolio.
What it means
A tight range means the caption holds one safe position instead of jumping frame to frame. Ours: 210px range. The comparison clip: 494–1644px.
02

Audio loudness

Measured with
ffmpeg -af loudnorm=print_format=summary — reports integrated loudness (LUFS) and true peak (dBTP) for the finished mix.
Measured on
Every clip we ship, and the same comparison clip.
What it means
Broadcast norm caps true peak at −1 dBTP. Ours: −14 LUFS, no clipping. The comparison clip clipped its peak.

Competitor left un-named: the numbers are checkable against the method above, a name isn't the point.
