PRIVACY POLICY

What this site does with your data

Short version: this page is static — no sign-up form, no contact form, no cookies set by it. Below is exactly what's true, not a standard template.

Who runs this site

Anton Fedotenkov, trading as Anton ReelForge. Contact for anything on this page, including data questions: Telegram @anton_fedotenkov.

What this site collects automatically

Nothing. There's no form here and no cookie set by this site. The only interactive element is the "Send footage" link, which opens a chat in Telegram — that's Telegram's product, not this site's, so anything you send there is covered by Telegram's own privacy policy, not this one.

What happens to footage sent over Telegram

It's used only to edit the clip you asked for. It isn't published or shown to anyone else without asking you first, and it isn't passed on to any third party.

Analytics and tracking

None on this site — no Google Analytics, no Vercel Analytics, no tracking pixel, no ad scripts. Checked directly in this page's source, not assumed.

The one thing this policy doesn't control: the site is hosted on Vercel, and like any web host, its servers keep basic request logs (IP address, timestamp, user agent) for running and securing the infrastructure. That's standard for any website on any host, nothing added on top of it, and nothing we look at ourselves.

Deleting your data

Message @anton_fedotenkov on Telegram and whatever you sent gets deleted.
